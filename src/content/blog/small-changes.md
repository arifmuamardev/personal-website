Lately, life has been feeling like a new chapter for me. I’m back to studying again, and everything around me feels a little different—new routines, new places, and plenty of things to learn outside the classroom too.

One small change I didn’t expect is how much walking has become part of my daily life. I walk almost everywhere now. To campus, to the store, to the station, and sometimes just to get some fresh air and reset my mind.

At first, it felt tiring. I kept thinking about distance, and my body needed time to adjust. But day by day, it started to feel normal. Walking gives me quiet time—time to think, to observe, and to slow down even when life feels busy.

Honestly, I think this habit is making me healthier. Not in a dramatic way, but in a simple and steady way. More movement, less rushing, and a little more balance in the middle of a busy schedule.
