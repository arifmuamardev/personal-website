Life has a way of keeping us on our toes, doesn’t it? Recently, I took a leap and applied for a program that could open doors to the next phase of my education. While I won’t share specifics just yet, I’m thrilled to share that I’ve been shortlisted as a candidate!

Between juggling work, studies, and family life, this waiting period has taught me to embrace the journey, even when the destination isn’t clear. To anyone else in a similar “limbo,” hang in there. Sometimes the not knowing is where we learn the most.

Fingers crossed, and stay tuned for updates!
