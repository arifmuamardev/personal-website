What to compare before choosing

None of the mockups is a final homepage. Evaluate how naturally they express a researcher's credibility and hands-on software work.

A — Academic MinimalBest for bibliography, CV and direct links; intentionally modest visual identity.
B — Researcher–EngineerBest balance of research, publications, code, writing and approachable personal identity.
C — Solid EditorialStrongest visual presence, but can feel more like an institutional website.

Working recommendation: B. The final decision should follow review on desktop and mobile.
